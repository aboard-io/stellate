STELLATE · accessible text version

A generative instrument you play by moving through a space of 273 musical genres. Every song is rendered live in your browser and is fully deterministic from a seed — the same seed always plays the same music. This version needs no vision: pick a genre or build a journey, and read what's playing as text. Your choices ride the address bar, so bookmarking or sharing the URL captures this exact music. Switch to the visual star-map version. · colophon
