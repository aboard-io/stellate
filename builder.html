Vaporwave Song Builder

Built on the Royal Road progression, rendered live in your browser by Csound (WebAssembly). Add audio by URL, stack layers, and export a WAV, an MP3, or the Csound source itself. Nothing is uploaded — audio is fetched and rendered client-side. Serve this folder over http://localhost (see serve.sh); press Play (a click is required to start audio).
