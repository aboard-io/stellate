the orbiteach row is a section · tap one to make it the seed

The document is 24 bits — sixteen for the row, eight for the rule. Everything else (kit, bass cell, melody cell, chord walk, and the sections) is a pure function of those bits, read through a lens per track. You manipulate the rule system; the sections are interpretations, never edits. See docs/CA.md.
