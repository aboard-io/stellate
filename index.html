Stellate

A generative instrument you play by moving through a 178-genre vector space. Every song is rendered live in your browser and is fully deterministic from a seed — the same seed always plays the same music. Every genre is verified by a genre-confusion matrix: all 178 must sound most like themselves after every change.

How to play

Drag anywhere to set a destination — the song journeys there, never jumps. Double-click to drop path waypoints: a constellation line connects them and a traveler walks the path, morphing the music through every genre it crosses — the path is the mix. Drag waypoints to move them, right-click to erase. Set the pace in the panel. Pinch (or ctrl+scroll) zooms the chart. ▶ plays, ⓘ opens “inside the sound” (the live voice timeline), ▢ the background, ⚙ the controls.
