LIBRETTO

The words of the song, written down. One line = one rule. The kernel already calls an operator list a word and a bar schedule “a sentence you can print, diff, serialize and hold a gate to” (kernel.js:1160–1176). Libretto is that sentence given a spelling — the whole Development axis, plus the seams it touches, as text you can type.

deterministic — no dice, seeds are named extracted — every verb is a kernel call that already ships one owner — one table emits chips, keys and words reaches the sound — a line that changes nothing is a parse error
voice 2 in odd : rotate 4·S · flip acc — the acid answer, verbatim. Today it is a closure only genres.js can say (genres.js:505). As a line, a saved song, a gate, or Paul can say it too.

01 · WHERE IT COMES FROM

Extracted, not invented

Four mechanisms in the kernel already ARE this language — they just have no spelling a human can type. Libretto adds the spelling and nothing below it: the parser’s entire output type is the OPKEYS alphabet that shipped in kernel.js:1176, resolved by asOps (kernel.js:1190). A rule the kernel cannot already perform is a parse error, not a feature request.

The word

Per-voice, per-section operator list. Today a closure: word: (v,s) => […]. Libretto’s subject + section selector is exactly the (v,s) pair, said out loud.

word: (v, s) =>
  v===0 ? [] : [rotate(4*s)]

The bar schedule

Position inside the section, cyclic. Already data: op keys per bar, resolved by periodOps.

The ghost layer

A word applied to one vector of a shadow copy — the only discipline (kernel.js:125) that keeps a subject recognizable.

genres.js:500

The pipes

Post-render event rewriters — strum, harmonize, echo, breathe. Total (unknown id = skipped) and seeded, mulberry32, never Math.random.

02 · THE GRAMMAR

One line, three clauses

WHO · WHEN · WHAT. The subject picks the voice(s) — the kernel’s v. The selector picks the section and bar — the kernel’s s and the period index. The word is operators left to right, exactly word()’s reduce (kernel.js:195). Everything else is glue.

subject when? : op · op …
└──────── the word: ops repeat, applied left → right ────────┘
op verb expr? on vector? seed name?

Rounded = terminal, square = expands below. on vector is the only() combinator worn as a suffix; seed is legal only on a pipe verb.

rule ::= subject { when } ":" word -- one line, one rule subject ::= "voice" n | "voices" n ".." [n] | "all" | "ghost" | part part ::= "lead" | "pads" | "bass" | "kit" -- the cast’s part names when ::= "in" section | "bar" n ["of" n] | "every" n section ::= role | "odd" | "even" | "S%"n "=" n -- role: intro verse chorus … word ::= "as written" | op { "·" op } op ::= verb [expr] ["on" vector] ["seed" name] verb ::= rotate reverse invert up down split delete thin fill | loop … from … spread flip copy … to … -- the algebra | strum harmonize echo breathe -- the pipes (seeded) expr ::= n { ("·"|"+"|"−"|"%") (n | counter) } counter ::= "S" | "B" | "V" -- section · bar · voice. Counters, never dice. vector ::= "gate" | "deg" | "acc" | "sld" | "vel" -- the pattern’s own vectors

04 · PROOF BY CATALOG

Eight rules the shelf already plays

Each card is a real catalog fact restated as lines. The extraction gate: compile the lines, render the score, diff it byte-for-byte against the closure’s score — test the artifact, not the source.

voice 1 : as written
voice 2 : rotate 4·S
voice 2 in odd : + flip acc
Acid. The sequencer never varies; the answer walks past itself 4 steps per section, flipping accents on the odd ones. The counter S is the whole closure’s arithmetic. A bare + appends to the voice’s previous rule.
word: (v,s) => v===0 ? [] : [rotate(4*s), ...(s%2 ? [complement("acc")] : [])] genres.js:505
voices 2.. : rotate 4 on gate · thin 3
New wave. The answer a bar late and thinned — re-time the rhythm without touching a note, then mask a third of the gates.
word: (v,s2) => v===0 ? [] : [only("gate", rotate(4)), drop(3)] genres.js:544
all : loop 8 from 4·S
Vaporwave. The loop-a-fragment move: keep 8 of 16 steps, the window sliding a bar per section. The catalog’s only use of the lossy operator, now one readable line.
word: (v,s) => [excerpt((s*4)%16, 8)] genres.js:595
voice 2 : up 3
voice 2 in S%3=1 : up 3 · reverse
voice 3 : reverse
Fugue (three of its twelve cells). The 4-voice × 3-column answer table becomes twelve one-line rules — the whole exposition legible at a glance instead of a nested array literal.

The bar schedule. Every fourth bar breathes. This is periodOps’ cyclic index worn as words — and it already round-trips as data (["thin3"]).

ghost : rotate 3 on acc
The ghost layer. Accents alone, walked against an unrotated gate — the shadow drummer. on acc is only(), the one-vector discipline.

05 · NEW POWER

Three sentences the kernel is one small verb away from

Each needs exactly one new entry in the algebra or one seam already named in fields.js — never a new mechanism. They answer the musicologist round’s structural gaps: real row technique, real hocket, sections that arrive.

# a named row is Material; using it is Development
row wien = 0 11 3 4 8 7 9 5 6 1 2 10
voice 2 in bridge : pitches row wien RI5
NEWSerialism. P/R/I/RI are reverse (kernel.js:93) and invert (kernel.js:100) the kernel already owns; the only new op is only("deg", rowmap) — read degrees through a named series. Rows live beside motifs in the bank: material, one owner.
voices 1,2 : hocket 2
NEWHocket. One line splits its gates alternately across two voices — medieval, and also every grime vocal chop. The first cross-voice word: today word(v,s) sees one voice at a time, so this needs the voice pair at the asOps seam. Desugar: voice 1 gets thin 2, voice 2 gets flip gate · thin 2 offset — both shipped ops (kernel.js:101,187).
chorus : sweep cut 400→2600 over 8 bars
NEWPer-section automation. fields.js already splits transitions into LEVEL (the event stream) and MOTION — “automation on the section’s mixer channel” (fields.js:95). Today MOTION is enum presets; this names the curve. Subject is a section, not a voice — the one place the grammar widens, and the widening is the seam fields.js drew first.

07 · DETERMINISM

No dice anywhere

Counters, not randomness

S, B, V are the section, bar and voice indices the kernel already passes to word(v,s) and periodOps. Expressions over them are arithmetic on position — the same line renders the same score forever.

Seeds are named

The only probabilistic-looking word, at .4, is a pipe’s coin — mulberry32, “Every pipe is TOTAL and SEEDED” (kernel.js:497). Libretto requires the seed in the line (seed hands) or it is 0. Reseed by retyping a word, never by reloading a page.

Total, like the algebra

Pattern in, valid pattern out (kernel.js:43). An unknown verb is a red line, and a red line is a skipped line — the song keeps playing, matching how asOps already treats an unknown chip: “silently nothing” (kernel.js:1189).

08 · DECISIONS THAT NEED WORDS

Annotations for the build

  1. One owner, three surfaces. The op alphabet lives twice today — fields.js:61 (chips) and kernel.js:1176 (keys) — held equal only by solo-shape.test.js. Libretto would be a third copy unless the build inverts it: one table in kernel.js emits chips, keys and verbs, and fields.js is pointed at it, which kernel.js:1170 already asks for in as many words.
  2. Where the lines live. song.libretto: an array of strings on the record, validated by song.js the same paranoid way as every field — typed errors {path, got, want}, line number as the path. The genre’s own closures remain the defaults; a libretto line shadows them per voice, which is exactly the take law’s shape: the box proposes, the hand overrides, provenance kept.
  3. Drum phrases pass through. A word over a drum phrase is the identity (kernel.js:195). The editor greys a melodic verb aimed at kit instead of erroring — the line is legal, it just says nothing yet, and greying is the dead-word law made visible.
  4. The axes police the vocabulary. A libretto line may only say Development and its two named seams (pipes, MOTION). Time words error with the law quoted; Sound words point at the desk. The error messages are how new users learn the eight axes without reading AXES.md.
  5. The extraction gate ships with it. For every catalog genre with a word/period/ghost/pipes: print its libretto, re-compile, render both scores, diff bytes. 122 genres become 122 fixtures, and the printer is the proof the language covers the catalog — TEST THE ARTIFACT, not the grammar.
