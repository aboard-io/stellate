The One / Board

One console, one set of effects, and the section moves on its face. Every mix fact the record owns is on this surface and nowhere else: the engineer's per-voice pots, the two board tables, the character chain, and the desk's hidden per-section shading all fold into it. Every value is a word from fields.js — detented, saved with the song, deterministic.

II · Section automation 6

“Different automation settings per song section — some voices raise and some fall.” Both views below are one fact: the grid is where you set (six words, saved with the song), the lanes are where you read — drawn from the same table, like the mini-lane on every strip. Dim is derived, bright is set.

tap any cell to cycle its word — the lanes and the strips follow

vocabulary: out · hush · back · — (as mixed) · fwd · lift   /   a word is a trim on the strip's fader for that section — deterministic, diffable, thumb-sized at 390px. A hand-drawn curve is what this table renders, never what you store.

III · What this board absorbs

The argument for the artifact: five surfaces and one hidden layer become one console. Each card names the surface that closes and where its facts now live.

1 The engineer's pots come home

The per-voice level / place / delay / room pots inside each voice's sheet in the band grid fold into the channel strip. A cantor's send stays a fact about the cantor — the sheet keeps a read-only mirror that links here — but the strip is the one owner and the one place that writes.

closes ui/engineer.js “THE ENGINEER” (per-voice pots in the band grid)

2 Two tables become one console

The channels table and the returns-and-main table at the foot of the page merge into one scroll. The two laws survive the merge: down a strip reads in signal order, across the deck a row is still a comparison — and the empty-cell problem G13 measured is gone because a strip only draws the rows it has.

closes ui/engineer.js VIEW 2 (two <table>s, desk-gate G13)

3 One set of effects

The record's master.fx character <select multiple> (hold-⌘ to pick a second) becomes three chain slots on every bus — three plain dropdowns, in series, slot order = signal order. “—” empties a slot; all-empty = the return exactly as the engine builds it. An insert costs a multiple, a bus costs a constant — so character lives on buses, never on instruments.

closes the character multi-select under the board (master.fx); fields.js FX stays the one table

4 Character is dealt, not embedded

“Maybe we don't embed it any more?” — right. A genre stops carrying g.fx chips and tone.verb bleed inside its recipe. It arrives instead as send amounts plus a chain preset per bus, written onto this board by extraction — visible, overridable, and saved as the song's own words. Nothing rides hidden on an instrument.

closes per-genre embedded fx/verb (genres.js recipes) → dealt to bus strips at compose

5 One record level

The transport bar's volume slider stays what it already is — the room, not the record, not saved with the song — and stops looking like a second master. The record's own level is the main fader on this board and nowhere else. One fact, one owner.

keeps the bar slider as the room; closes its claim on the record's level

6 Nothing moves that the board doesn't draw

The desk's per-section level lanes, section EQ and seat shading move the mix today with no surface. The automation panel puts those numbers on the face — and the same law runs the other way: a knob that reaches no sound is not drawn. Width and tilt appear on this mock because the ideal wires them; today they round-trip and reach nothing (audio/desk.js names all three and says why).

surfaces audio/desk.js section chains · enforces “no knob that cannot reach the sound”

Laws honored: every value is a word from fields.js tables (deterministic, no free floats); absent is the only spelling of a default; the fill is the automation, the number is your offset; dim is derived, bright is set; the track list is desk-doc's channelVoicesOf — a board cannot show a strip the desk will not build; and the section grid is score-level data, so gates read it off the rendered record, not this page. Cast drawn to the desk's own chair law: chairs + bass + drums, instruments by pool-first resolution.
