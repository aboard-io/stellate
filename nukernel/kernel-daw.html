Song

Click a box to play from there; double-click to loop it alone. Drag boxes to reorder. Trim the right edge for length, the left to nudge into the form.

Phrases

Click a phrase to edit it and switch it on or off in the selected box. Several at once is fine — the genre deals them across its voices, so two phrases in a fugue is a double fugue.

Editing phrase 1

Click deg, oct and vel to raise, shift-click to lower — deg spans −7…+7, oct −2…+2, vel 0…9. Click gate / acc / sld to toggle. vel is how loud; acc is the 303 accent, which opens the filter and is what operators key on.
