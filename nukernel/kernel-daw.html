Phrases

Eight slots, all empty to start. Pick one, then Seed, Random or Clear it. The selected slot is what plays.

Slot editor

Click deg, oct and vel to raise, shift-click to lower — deg spans −7…+7, oct −2…+2, vel 0…9. Click gate / acc / sld to toggle. vel is how loud; acc is the 303 accent, which opens the filter and is what operators key on.
