Editing phrase 1

Tap a value cell for its fader, or drag up and down on it to scrub — sideways (or a second finger) for fine. Tap a gate/acc/sld key to flip it. vel is level; acc is the 303 accent. On a phone the pattern reads top to bottom like a tracker — every numbered row is a 16th, and RAMP shows the inc/stk columns. Phrases below: tap one to edit it and switch it on or off in the selected box; [+] adds a phrase, [−] removes the last one.

Phrases

Click one to edit it and switch it on or off in the selected box. [+] adds a phrase (up to 16); [−] removes the last one when no box plays it.

Song

Click to play from a box, double-click to loop it, hold to read everything it has switched on, drag to reorder, trim the edges. Genres stack — the first owns the harmony, drums and tempo; the rest ride on top of it, each with its own phrases. Every box is also a mixer channel: its own effects, its own reverb and echo sends, its own way in and out.
