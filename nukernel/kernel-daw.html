Mix

A mixing board: one strip per sound the selected box makes, read from the same track list the audio builds its channels from. Tap a strip's value keys — effects, reverb, echo, level, place — for that sound's chips; M mutes it and S solos it (a solo anywhere in the box silences everything not soloed). The fader is automated: while the box plays, the cap shows the level actually driving that channel, and dragging it stores an offset that rides on top — the automation keeps moving under your trim, shown in dB under the slot. The section strip, under the rule, is the whole box, which every part feeds — it also chooses which room the reverb is and the echo's subdivision; the bus strips after it are the page's shared reverb, echo and drum room, and the master strip meters the sum. The rack below is one knob row per bus plus the master-bus row — every knob detented, absent meaning the graph exactly as built.
